SUPPORT — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The essentials on our products, testing, ordering, and delivery. Still need a hand? Contact us.

Are these products for human use?

No. All products are chemical reagents supplied exclusively for laboratory research and development. They are not for human or veterinary consumption and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Do you provide a certificate of analysis?

Yes — every batch ships with a certificate of analysis. Each production lot is sent to an accredited third-party lab and released only against HPLC purity (≥ 99%) and mass-spectrometry identity confirmation.

Where are your peptides made?

Every peptide is synthesized and lyophilized in United States facilities under controlled conditions, then independently verified for purity and identity before release.

How do I pay for my order?

No payment is collected on the site. After placing your order you'll receive an order number and Venmo details. Send the amount shown to {{ venmoHandle }} and paste the provided order note so we can match your payment — your order is prepared once payment is received.

How much is shipping and how fast is it?

A flat cold-chain rate of $15.00 is added to every order, with taxes included. Orders are dispatched within 48 hours of payment in temperature-controlled packaging. See Shipping for details.

Do you ship internationally?

Not at this time — we ship to US addresses only. Checkout will warn you if an address doesn't look like a US state.

Can an order be rejected or refunded?

Occasionally, yes. In the off chance an order can't be filled, a full refund is given (Venmo fees are not reimbursed).

Why do I need a referral password?

The catalog is available to referred researchers. Enter the password you were given to browse and order. If you don't have one, check with the person who referred you.
